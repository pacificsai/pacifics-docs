# FAQ

## What is Pacifics?

A security context graph and attack-path intelligence platform that helps you
find and prioritize real, exploitable risk.

## What access does Pacifics need?

Read-only access to the environments you connect. See [Integrations](../integrations/integrations.md).

## How are attack paths prioritized?

By reachability and the value of the target asset. Paths that reach sensitive
assets in few hops rank highest.

## How is my data handled?

Data is used to build your private context graph and power AI reasoning
grounded in your environment.
