# Quickstart

Get Pacifics running against your environment in a few steps.

## Prerequisites

- A Pacifics account and workspace.
- Read access to at least one supported environment (cloud, identity, or SCM).

## Steps

1. Sign in to your Pacifics workspace.
2. Connect an environment — see [Connect your environment](./connect-environment.md).
3. Wait for the initial graph build to complete.
4. Open the attack-path view and review your first findings.
