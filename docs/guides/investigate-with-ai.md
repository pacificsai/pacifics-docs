# Investigate with AI

Use AI reasoning to investigate a finding or attack path end to end.

## Steps

1. Open a finding or attack path.
2. Ask the AI to explain the risk and its root cause.
3. Review the grounded evidence pulled from your graph.
4. Follow the recommended remediation.

## Tips

- Ask follow-up questions to narrow scope.
- Pivot from an explanation directly into the graph to verify.
